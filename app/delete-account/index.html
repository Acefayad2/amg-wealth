Delete your account

You can request permanent deletion directly inside AMG App.

  1. Sign in to the AMG account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile → Account deletion. Account deletion is also available during licensing setup.
  3. Read the explanation, enter your current password, and confirm deletion.
Open account deletion

Deletion is permanent. It removes your account and associated app data. AMG App and AMG Portal share authentication, so deletion also removes the shared portal account and its associated records.

What happens to your information?

Associated app records are deleted with the account. Copies previously delivered to other people, such as emails or calendar invitations, may remain with those recipients. Limited security audit records and service backups may remain under the provider’s retention schedule.

Can’t sign in?

Try “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. If you still need assistance, email acefayad@gmail.com from your account email address. We may need to verify ownership before helping with deletion. Do not send your password.

See the app privacy policy for more about data use and your choices.
