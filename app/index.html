AMG App

Your day. Your contacts.
Your next step.

AMG App helps approved Anointed Money Group agents and trainers organize contacts, follow-ups, daily tasks, appointments, and training in one workspace.

Open AMG AppGet help with access

Access is for approved AMG agents and trainers. Creating an account does not automatically grant access to every feature. Your AMG coordinator manages approval and licensing access.

The iPhone app is in testing. An App Store download link will be added when available.

Organize your activity

Keep contact notes, review today’s calendar and tasks, and plan your next follow-up.

Prepare and coordinate

Access scripts and licensing preparation, record activity, and coordinate appointments with trainers.

Reminders inside AMG

This release shows reminders inside the app. Open AMG to review them. Phone push alerts and appointment emails are not available in this release.

AMG App is a business organization and training tool. Licensing preparation does not replace required education or confer a license.
