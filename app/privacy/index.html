AMG privacy policy

Updated October 27, 2026. Anointed Money Group operates AMG App for agent business organization, training, and licensing preparation.

Information you provide

We store your account name, email, phone, team, role, and optional profile photo. We also store the prospects, contact details, notes, activities, goals, guest invitations, appointment details, practice self-reviews, and licensing progress that you enter. Your password is handled by our authentication provider.

Device permissions

You can optionally choose a profile photo. This version does not request access to your location. Script rehearsal does not record your microphone or create an audio recording.

Optional notes dictation uses your browser or device speech service, which may send audio to its provider for transcription. AMG does not store dictation audio. Transcribed text is stored as contact notes only when you save it. You can type instead or stop dictation at any time.

Optional phone-contact follow-ups require Full Access to Contacts on iPhone. AMG checks when it is open or returns to the foreground. Existing contacts are skipped on connection; identifiers are hashed and kept on your phone to detect new additions. Newly detected names, phone numbers, and email addresses are saved to your private Prospects with a follow-up one hour after detection. Turning this feature off stops imports; previously saved prospects remain until you delete them. No contacts are messaged automatically.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to operate your account, synchronize your work, provide training, manage licensing approval, and coordinate appointments. AMG administrators and licensing coordinators can view account and licensing information needed for their roles. Rankings show agents’ names, teams, and activity totals to signed-in approved agents. Prospect details are private to their account.

Supabase provides authentication, database, and photo storage. Netlify hosts the app. Appointment details are shared with the selected trainer and the AMG Matchup service. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, appointment information is used for that calendar integration. Service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use the app for advertising tracking.

This release provides in-app reminders only. We store reminder preferences, time zone, and notification history to show reminders inside AMG. Notification history is retained for up to 90 days. You can turn reminders off or select quiet hours in the app. Phone push alerts and appointment emails are not available in this release. Authentication emails, including account verification and password resets, are separate.

Before each AI message draft, we ask for your permission to send your agent name, this contact’s first name, saved notes, status, product interest, source, follow-up date, and recent activity types and dates through Netlify AI Gateway to OpenAI to generate suggestions. You can decline and keep using AMG without AI drafting. Their phone number and email fields are excluded, but personal information written in notes may still be shared. Review notes before using this feature and remove sensitive or unauthorized information. Drafts are not automatically sent or saved to your contact record. Usage counts are retained for 30 days to control access and cost.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your profile and app records. Use Account deletion from Profile or licensing setup to permanently remove your account and associated app data. Because AMG App and AMG Portal share authentication, deletion also removes that portal account and its associated records. Copies already delivered to other people, such as emails and calendar invitations, may remain with those recipients. Limited security audit records and service backups may remain under the provider’s retention schedule.

Only enter contact information you are authorized to use. Do not enter passwords, government identifiers, medical records, or payment card information in notes or practice scripts.
