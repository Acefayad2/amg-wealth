Get back into your account

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen to reset your password. For approval or licensing access, contact your AMG coordinator.

Report an issue

Include your app version, device model, and a short description of what happened. If you include a screenshot, remove personal or client information first. Never email passwords, verification codes, identity documents, or client financial or medical information.

Where are my reminders?

Open AMG to check your notifications, tasks, and appointments. This release uses in-app reminders only; it does not send phone push alerts or appointment emails. Account verification and password-reset emails are separate.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the app privacy policy or follow the account-deletion instructions. You can also email support for privacy, access, correction, or deletion assistance.
