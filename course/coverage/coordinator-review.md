# AMG Maryland Life — coordinator review edition

September 12, 2026. Maryland Life Producer, Series 20–27. All 60 teaching parts are produced. This record distinguishes production checks from licensing-coordinator approval, which has not been recorded.

## Available instruction

- 60 short video lessons, with 512 required website questions and explanatory feedback for every option. Every question must be answered correctly after any needed review before the learner can finish the part. First answers and retries are retained separately.
- Actual teaching and question evidence for all 307 AMG teaching groups across the 48 numbered outline subsections. The 307 figure is an internal organization of the official outline, not an official count of exam questions.
- Written teaching, applied scenarios, source links, captions, saved playback, mobile question dialogs and a transcript study alternative. Learning progress is local to the browser; this is not a central employer compliance transcript or an accredited CE record.
- Maryland law and federal tax assumptions are dated and distinguished from fictional contract examples. Sources and corrections are recorded in full-course/source-verification.md.

The 60-part curriculum expands the former two-part prototype. Black and Hispanic clients and professionals appear in the scenarios, with other backgrounds represented as appropriate. Narrated scenes and native diagrams carry the teaching; questions and their feedback are website interactions. The AMG circular mark is in the lower-left video safe area.

## Production acceptance

Each new video received whole-file decode and duration checks, review of all narration sections against the written script, and visual inspection of every teaching layout. Speech faults and crowded labels identified during review were corrected and checked again. Individual media manifests and player-qa files record the evidence. Automated scene analysis is an additional check; it can misdescribe still-image activity and is not a substitute for the other checks. These records do not claim that a human watched every minute in real time or that a future browser cannot fail.

All lesson completion flows were tested, including native video-ended events, four-option feedback, retry history, saved completion, playback resume, captions and mobile layout. Parts 1–2 now use the shared player while retaining their interactive examples.

## Available assessments

The independent 32-question diagnostic, three 90-question timed forms, review schedule and progress dashboard are available. The 302 reserved assessment items are distinct from the 512 required lesson questions. Each timed form contains 80 scored questions plus 10 AMG simulation items, with 105 minutes and no feedback before submission. Simulation items are excluded from the main score and identified during review. They are original AMG items, not actual Prometric experimental questions.

The currently linked official Life outline specifies scored domain counts of 24, 8, 14, 8, 11, 7, 6 and 2. Its older filename is not a new 2026 revision. Official outline: https://www.prometric.com/files/mia/2027_life_producer_9.21.21.pdf

Keep fresh attempts separate from retakes. Two previously unseen forms scoring at least 85% on separate days are a proposed AMG study target, not an official cut score or validated pass predictor. The dashboard labels samples under 20 fresh scored answers as limited evidence. A high score on a few questions is not a domain-mastery finding. Some low-weight domains will still have small samples after all three forms. Review mistakes and uncertain answers before consuming another fresh form. First-exposure scores, retakes, original mistakes and explanation acknowledgments are separate. Delayed review uses another original question from the related lesson where available, at 1-, 3- and 7-day intervals; repeat practice is labeled and is not counted as fresh exam evidence.
