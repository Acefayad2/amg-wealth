The learning and review sequence

  1. Understand it. Plain-language teaching, definitions and a client example.
  2. Apply it. Four-option questions, believable distractors and explanations for every choice.
  3. Remember it. New scenarios after a delay; review guesses and weak topics.
  4. Test it. Three independent timed forms, with no feedback during the attempt.
  5. Check readiness. Proposed AMG target: 85% on two fresh forms, plus stronger evidence in each domain. These targets are not an official cut score or a guarantee.
