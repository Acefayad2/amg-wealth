About this practice material

AMG original study material · Maryland Accident & Health. Scores describe these practice items; they are not an official exam result or a guarantee of passing.

The exam has 80 scored questions plus 10 unscored, 1 hour 45 minutes, weighted: Maryland regulation 30%, General insurance 9%, Health basics 11%, Policy provisions 8%, Disability income 9%, Medical plans 14%, Group health 5%, Dental 1%, Seniors and special needs 9%, Federal tax 5%.
