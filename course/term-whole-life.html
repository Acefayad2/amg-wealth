LESSON 06 / POLICY FOUNDATIONS

A deadline or a lifetime?

Learn the difference between term and traditional whole life through client scenarios, visual explanations, and three checks of your understanding.

TERM

A specified period

Ordinary term provides protection for a defined period and has no cash value. Comparable coverage generally starts at a lower premium than whole life.

TRADITIONAL WHOLE LIFE

A permanent design

Designed for permanent protection and guaranteed cash value under the contract. Required premiums and other policy conditions still matter.
