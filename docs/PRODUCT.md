# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: families, young professionals, business owners and pre-retirees who are starting to think about life insurance, retirement and leaving something behind. Many arrive on a phone from Instagram, a referral, or an event, often unsure what they need and wary of being sold to. Secondary: current and prospective AMG agents who use Resources and Licensing for training, exam prep, events and the business opportunity.

## Product Purpose

anointedmoneygroup.com exists to turn curiosity into a booked consultation. Success is a visitor who understands, in plain language, how AMG can protect their family and build wealth, trusts Simi and Zamilah, and submits the consultation form (or calls). Agent-facing pages (Resources, Licensing, Matchups) should make next steps obvious without hunting.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, ambitious. The energy of a packed arena stage: momentum, culture, belief that ordinary families can build generational wealth. Confident and celebratory, but never pushy. Emerald and gold carry the identity; the leaders' real photos and stage moments carry the energy.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI or SaaS landing-page templates: identical card grids, tracked eyebrows on every section, stock-photo smiles.
- Cluttered or busy pages: too many effects, animations or sections competing for attention.
- Cold corporate banks: stiff, impersonal, jargon-heavy copy and grey institutional layouts.

## Design Principles

1. One clear next step: every section moves the visitor toward booking a consultation (or, on agent pages, toward the right resource).
2. Real people, real moments: Simi, Zamilah and AMG events over stock imagery or abstract decoration.
3. Plain language over finance jargon: explain, then invite.
4. Energy with restraint: bold type, color and motion used where they earn attention, not everywhere.
5. Readable for everyone: phone-first, high contrast, nothing important hidden behind hover or animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many visitors are older or on phones: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, visible focus states, full keyboard access, touch targets ≥44px, and every animation has a reduced-motion alternative. Content must be visible without JavaScript-triggered reveals.
