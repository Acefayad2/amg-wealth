# AMG weekly schedule flyer

Created using the built-in image generation tool. The original schedule and the existing AMG logo were supplied as reference images.

## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: polished, shareable AMG weekly schedule flyer, landscape format approximately 1.65:1, high resolution and very legible text.
Input image 1 is the source schedule to redesign. Input image 2 is the actual AMG logo to use, preserving its exact circular interlocking gold monogram. Replace the large SOS lion branding, red palette, brush lettering, and busy lightning with AMG branding. Keep all event days, times, titles, and locations exactly as transcribed below. This is a design change, not a schedule change.
Design: premium financial-services brand, near-black background, rich emerald panels, warm metallic gold fine rules and accents, ivory readable type. Sophisticated restrained Bodoni-style serif for the large WEEKLY SCHEDULE heading and elegant clean sans-serif for all day headings, times and event details. The real circular AMG mark at upper left with ANOINTED MONEY GROUP next to it, beautifully spaced. Flat front-facing finished graphic, no mockup surroundings. Avoid gritty textures, lions, shields, red, brush typography, excessive glow, extra slogans or invented events. Fine subtle background texture and a few restrained geometric gold details are welcome.
Layout: generous compact header with AMG branding and WEEKLY SCHEDULE; six clearly separated equal columns MONDAY through SATURDAY across the width, aligned day headers, roomy event blocks with gold times, white event names and soft gold locations. Simple restrained small outline icons can accompany events without compromising text space. FRIDAY stays blank underneath its heading; do not invent events. A separate, clearly Monday-specific gold-accented notice along the bottom preserves the special monthly call. Keep all typography crisp, balanced, and comfortably inside the edges, no text collisions.
