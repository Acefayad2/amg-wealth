Which strategy fits where you are?

The right mix depends on your age, goals, and budget. Here's a plain-language look at the tools we use most.

Term life insurance

Coverage for a set number of years, often 10, 20, or 30. It's usually the most affordable way to protect a young family through the years when they depend on your income most.

Many term policies can later be converted to permanent coverage, depending on the carrier.

Ask about term coverage →
  • LengthFixed term
  • CostLowest to start
  • Cash valueNone
  • Best forIncome replacement

Whole life insurance

Permanent coverage with level premiums and a guaranteed cash value that grows over time. It's a foundation for families who want protection that never expires.

Cash value can typically be borrowed against for opportunities or emergencies.

Ask about whole life →
  • LengthLifetime
  • CostHigher, level premiums
  • Cash valueGuaranteed growth
  • Best forLifelong protection

Indexed universal life (IUL)

Permanent coverage whose cash value is credited based on a market index, with a floor that helps protect against losses. Premiums can flex as life changes.

Often used as part of a tax-advantaged wealth and retirement strategy.

Ask about IUL →
  • LengthLifetime
  • CostFlexible premiums
  • Cash valueIndex-linked, with a floor
  • Best forProtection plus growth

Annuities

Contracts that turn savings into dependable income, now or later. Fixed and indexed annuities can help protect principal while you plan for retirement.

Common for 401(k) rollovers and anyone who wants income they can't outlive.

Ask about annuities →
  • PurposeRetirement income
  • FundingLump sum or over time
  • GrowthFixed or index-linked
  • Best forPre-retirees & retirees

Living benefits

Riders that let you access part of your death benefit early if you face a qualifying chronic, critical, or terminal illness, so a policy can help you while you're still here.

Availability and terms vary by carrier and policy.

Ask about living benefits →
  • TypePolicy rider
  • TriggersQualifying illness
  • PayoutPart of death benefit
  • Best forProtecting your own future

What happens in a consultation?

We start with Discovery: a private conversation about your income, family, assets, debts, and goals. You leave with a clear picture of where you stand and a recommended next step. There's no obligation to buy anything.

Who does AMG work with?

Families, young professionals, business owners, and pre-retirees. If you have people who depend on you or a future you want to protect, we can help you plan for it.

Which insurance companies do you work with?

As a proud partner of Integrity Marketing Group, we shop coverage across carriers such as Athene, North American, F&G, Pacific Life, Mutual of Omaha, Nationwide, AIG, and more, so the recommendation fits you rather than one company.

How much life insurance do I need?

It depends on your income, debts, children, and existing savings. Try the coverage estimator for a starting number, then we'll refine it together.

Do I need a medical exam?

Not always. Many carriers offer accelerated underwriting that relies on health history instead of an exam for eligible applicants. Requirements depend on the carrier, your age, and the amount of coverage.

Which states are you licensed in?

Our agents are licensed across 38 states and growing. Reach out and we'll confirm availability where you live.

Can I become an AMG agent?

Yes. Start by joining a Business Opportunity Meeting, then visit Licensing to begin the process.
